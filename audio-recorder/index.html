    • Wear headphones. On speakers, your mic also picks up the other people, so they’re recorded twice and sound echoey.
    • Phone call? Take it on your computer (Teams, a softphone) if you can. Otherwise put the phone on speaker next to the computer and record with the microphone.
    • Check the meters before the call starts. Each one should move when its side talks.
    • Tell people you’re recording. Teams won’t show them that this page is recording.
    • Long calls: Compressed uses about 60 MB per hour. WAV uses 5 to 10 times that.
